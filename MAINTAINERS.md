# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

**Madoka** is the primary maintainer of Pointer under PilkQuant.

Responsibilities include:

- release readiness and versioning;
- technical direction and maintenance triage;
- test, security, and evidence-quality standards;
- packaging and release-note preparation;
- post-release verification and rollback decisions.

Repository ownership and publication credentials remain with the PilkQuant GitHub/PyPI account. Account-level publication does not change Madoka's maintainer role.
